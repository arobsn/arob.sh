I've spent many years designing, discussing and building software — and, more importantly, learning from people who are generous enough to share what they know. Along the way, I've accumulated notes, opinions, and lessons that rarely made it beyond a pull request or a private conversation.

So, this is an attempt to fix that.

I wanted a small, quiet corner — somewhere to turn half-formed notes into ideas, write about things I'm building, and document some of the decisions behind them.

## Why now?

Honestly, I'm not entirely sure. Maybe I have a little more free time these days. Maybe I wanted an excuse to spend a weekend tweaking VitePress until it looked like a blog.

But I think the real reason is simpler: I've been thinking about writing for a while, and I wanted to give it a try.

Thanks for stopping by.
